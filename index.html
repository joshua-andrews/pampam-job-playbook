    01 Resume · work experience

    Build a bullet with STAR.

    For every job: title, company (hyperlinked), location, remote or in person, dates, one-line intro, then 2–3 bullets like this. Type below and watch it come together.

    S

    Situation

    What was going on?

    T

    Task

    What were you asked to do?

    A

    Action

    What did YOU do?

    R

    Result

    What changed? Use a number.

    02 Job Scan

    Robots read your resume first.

    An ATS (Applicant Tracking System) compares your resume to the job description and filters people out before any human sees it. Not every company uses one, but more do every year.

    08 · 09 Interviews

    Three rounds. Three different goals.

    08 The money question

    Have your number ready.

    They may ask early. Pick one specific number, not a range, and say the same number in every round.

    $—

    Your number (MXN / month)

    Just starting out? If their minimum covers what you need, take it. Experience buys you leverage for the next one.

    09 Final interview

    Think like the boss.

    "Are you going to make me money?"

    If you're this far, they want you. They're just looking for a reason to seal the deal. Fewer technical questions, more vibes and business sense.
